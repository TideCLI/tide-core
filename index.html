Intelligence-DrivenEngine for Terminal

TIDE is a powerful CLI tool that brings AI-powered coding assistance directly to your terminal. Built with Bun, powered by LLMs, designed for developers.

Why TIDE?

⚡

Blazing Fast

Engineered with Bun and Rust for near-instant performance in any environment.

🖥️

Terminal First

A sophisticated TUI that replaces complex web interfaces with raw terminal power.

🔗

Provider Agnostic

Connect to OpenAI, Anthropic, or local LLMs. Your keys, your choice, your control.

🧠

Autonomous

Not just a chat box. TIDE understands project architecture and executes complex tasks.

🔧

Tool Native

Deep integration with git, shell, and file systems. It doesn't just suggest; it builds.

📊

Observable

Full transparency with built-in analytics for token usage, latency, and cost tracking.
